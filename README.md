## Recurring series

Worth a calendar reminder even when the next date has not been announced.

- **[SREcon](https://www.usenix.org/srecon)** — USENIX. The reference SRE conference. Americas, EMEA and APAC editions.
- **[KubeCon + CloudNativeCon](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/)** — CNCF flagship. NA, Europe, Japan, China, India editions plus a large co-located day-zero programme.
- **[Kubernetes Community Days (KCD)](https://www.cncf.io/kcds/)** — CNCF community-run, city-scale, dozens per year worldwide. Cheapest way to speak for the first time.
- **[DevOpsDays](https://devopsdays.org/)** — the original distributed conference series. 60+ cities a year, most with open CFPs.
- **[Conf42](https://www.conf42.com/)** — free, online, multiple tracks a year (SRE, Observability, DevSecOps, Kubernetes, Platform Engineering, Chaos Engineering).
- **[SREday](https://sreday.com/)** — quarterly, single-track, city editions (London, NYC, Hyderabad, Amsterdam).
- **[Monitorama](https://monitorama.com/)** — monitoring and observability, Portland.
- **[All Day DevOps](https://www.alldaydevops.com/)** — free 24-hour online conference, tens of thousands of attendees.
- **[SCaLE](https://www.socallinuxexpo.org/)** — Southern California Linux Expo, large community track for ops and cloud native.
- **[FOSDEM](https://fosdem.org/)** — Brussels, free, February. Multiple ops-adjacent devrooms including SRE, monitoring and containers.
- **[Open Source Summit](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/)** — Linux Foundation, NA / Europe / Japan / China.
- **[ContainerDays](https://www.containerdays.io/)** — Hamburg and satellite editions.
- **[Incident Management / postmortem community events](https://www.usenix.org/srecon)** — usually run as SREcon tracks rather than standalone conferences.
